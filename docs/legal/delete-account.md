# Delete your Gridly account

**App:** Gridly (`com.gridlygames.app`)  
**Developer:** Shaunak Studios

## How to request account deletion

You can delete your account and associated cloud data using either method:

### Option 1 — In the app (recommended)

1. Open **Gridly**.
2. Go to **App settings** (gear on the home screen) → **Send feedback**.
3. Send a message titled **Account deletion request** and include the email address tied to your account.
4. We will delete your account and cloud data within **30 days** and confirm by email if you provided one.

### Option 2 — Email

Send an **Account deletion request** to the developer contact email shown on the [Gridly Google Play listing](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.gridlygames.app), including the email address used for your account.

## What is deleted

When your account is deleted, we remove:

- Your authentication account (email/password or Google-linked account)
- Your profile row in our database
- Synced game statistics (Word Hunt, Grid Snap, Color Flow)
- Synced app and game settings (theme, reminders, difficulty preferences)

## What is not deleted or may be retained

- **Feedback or bug reports you previously submitted** — may be kept for product improvement (contact email disassociated where possible).
- **Custom puzzle invites you created** — expire automatically after **90 days**; may remain until expiry.
- **Data on your device (guest play, local saves)** — stays on your device until you uninstall the app or clear app storage; not stored in our cloud if you never signed in.

## Guest users

If you played as a **guest** and never created an account, we do not hold an account or synced cloud data for you. Remove local data by uninstalling Gridly or clearing app storage in Android settings.
